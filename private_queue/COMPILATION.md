# Compilation readiness assessment

The existing scheduled private-queue workflow also runs `compilation_assessment.py` after its regular steps. This is read-only preparation assessment, not automatic compilation or publishing. The new step has a four-minute timeout and failure does not count as clearance. Its logs contain only counts and totals, not source identities, URLs, media, or review contents. The encrypted queue is never modified by this step.

Only signed full-audio reviews matching exact retrieved bytes and prepared-media fingerprints are counted; files must have both video and audio. Only ten minutes or more can be marked eligible for preparation. Already published Shorts remain eligible for a different compilation. The assessment checks a separate `compilation_attempts` ledger, if present, and excludes sources and hashes used by active or published compilation records. Unknown or missing attempt metadata stops assessment; an outstanding, scheduled or uncertain compilation blocks readiness. The function does not create, retry, reconcile, or persist any compilation attempt: production delivery cannot safely be enabled until those mechanisms exist and are tested against the actual provider.

The schedule is subject to the hosting service and is not a precise timer. This does not ensure source completeness, detect songs in unreviewed audio, create a content-specific title or thumbnail, render a real compilation, or upload and publish it. Do not treat a ready result as authorization or proof of any such steps. Never create audio attestations without actual explicit complete review.
