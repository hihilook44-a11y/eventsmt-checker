# Advisory music-risk assessment

The scheduled workflow downloads fixed, checksum-verified YAMNet and EfficientAT MN10 ONNX models and assesses up to one previously prepared audio candidate. It retains only source-bound digests, model version and scores in the encrypted queue; it never alters source audio. The two outcomes are `music_suspected` and `inconclusive`. Neither grants audio clearance or allows posting. The existing cryptographic exact-byte review gate and separate compilation duplicate/unknown-attempt protections stay in force. A classifier failure cannot grant clearance.

Local single-CPU test, 2026-11-11: the known nine-clip sample had one clip the user previously identified as containing a song and eight they previously described as song-free. At a peak signal threshold of 0.10 for either model, the known song was flagged, while 5/8 previously described song-free clips were also flagged. Of 12 artificial mixtures of the same known song with three speech clips at several levels, 2 were below this threshold. A previous filtered speech-plus-song case with residual song also scored below threshold. These small non-independent samples are not representative accuracy estimates; a low score cannot demonstrate absence of music. Test labels and source files are not published here.

Inference on the local one-CPU machine for the sample suite, including model load, took approximately 13 seconds and 163 MB peak RSS; GitHub startup, installation and network add time. The two files total about 39 MiB and are cached by the workflow. Model output indices and sample rate are taken from their model cards. The EfficientAT card inconsistently calls 160,000 samples at 32 kHz ten seconds; this implementation uses the actual input shape, i.e. five seconds at 32 kHz. Further validated data and model evaluation are required before considering any automated absence decision.

Model sources and licenses:
- YAMNet ONNX: https://huggingface.co/audiomagic/yamnet-onnx (see original YAMNet license and model card).
- EfficientAT MN10: https://huggingface.co/minhducjp/webdonhac-models (MIT for this model; AudioSet labels under CC BY-SA 4.0). Florian Schmid et al., "Efficient Large-Scale Audio Tagging via Transformer-to-CNN Knowledge Distillation", ICASSP 2023; see model card for full license text.

Additional local evaluation: PANNs Cnn14 ONNX (SHA-256 `be2badf3f285866f6b389ac8a27bf705a0f31a11b6f5ffbace51d81b4d4127a9`, same model-card source, CC BY 4.0) caught more quiet mixtures, but the YAMNet/PANNs pair still missed 1/12 controlled overlaps and the filtered residual case at 0.10, and flagged 7/8 previously described song-free clips. Runtime was approximately 25 seconds with 1.1 GiB peak RSS. It is not deployed in the scheduled pair: it did not establish a reliable song-absence decision and adds about 312 MiB of weights. These comparisons are limited tests, not proof that all automatic methods are unsuitable.
